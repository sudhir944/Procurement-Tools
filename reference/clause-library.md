# Default Clause Library (Template)

> **Starter templates only — not vetted legal language.** Replace with your organization's counsel-approved clauses. The `clause-library` skill uses this file only when no organization-specific library is supplied. Have a licensed attorney review any clause before use. Fill in every [PLACEHOLDER].

---

## Limitation of Liability — Preferred (mutual cap)
**Use when:** Establishing balanced liability. **Placeholders:** [PARTY A], [PARTY B], [CAP PERIOD].

> EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY [PARTY A] TO [PARTY B] IN THE [CAP PERIOD, e.g. TWELVE (12) MONTHS] PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE LIABILITY. "EXCLUDED CLAIMS" MEANS A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY, AND LIABILITY ARISING FROM GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT.

**Note:** Keep the cap and the carve-outs mutual. Do not let confidentiality or IP indemnity fall under the cap for the other side while remaining uncapped for you.

---

## Consequential Damages Waiver — Preferred (mutual)
**Placeholders:** none.

> EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR ANY LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR GOODWILL, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR OTHERWISE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

**Note:** Ensure this is mutual and that "Excluded Claims" is defined consistently with the liability cap.

---

## Indemnification — Fallback (vendor-to-customer, IP + third-party)
**Placeholders:** [VENDOR], [CUSTOMER].

> [VENDOR] will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless [CUSTOMER] from and against any third-party claims, and any resulting losses, damages, and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) [VENDOR]'s infringement or misappropriation of any third-party intellectual property right, or (b) [VENDOR]'s gross negligence or willful misconduct, provided that [CUSTOMER] promptly notifies [VENDOR], grants control of the defense, and reasonably cooperates.

**Note:** Do not concede a broad, uncapped indemnity flowing from Customer to Vendor. Keep procedure (notice, control, cooperation) intact.

## Confidentiality — Preferred (mutual)
**Placeholders:** [TERM YEARS].

> Each party (as "Recipient") will protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least reasonable care, use it solely to perform under this Agreement, and not disclose it except to personnel with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations. These obligations continue for [TERM YEARS, e.g. THREE (3)] years after disclosure, except that trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets. Confidential Information excludes information that is or becomes public through no fault of Recipient, was rightfully known before disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party.

**Note:** Avoid a "residuals" clause that lets the other side freely use your information from memory. Keep standard exclusions.

---

## Termination for Convenience — Preferred
**Placeholders:** [NOTICE DAYS].

> Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon [NOTICE DAYS, e.g. THIRTY (30)] days' prior written notice to the other party. Upon termination, [CUSTOMER] will pay for services properly performed through the effective date of termination, and each party will return or destroy the other's Confidential Information.

**Note:** Prefer a mutual right. If the counterparty resists, at minimum secure the right for your side.

---

## IP / Work Product — Preferred (customer owns deliverables)
**Placeholders:** [VENDOR], [CUSTOMER].

> All deliverables and work product created by [VENDOR] specifically for [CUSTOMER] under this Agreement ("Deliverables") are works made for hire and, to the extent they do not qualify as such, [VENDOR] hereby assigns to [CUSTOMER] all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables. [VENDOR] retains ownership of its pre-existing materials and tools ("Background IP") and grants [CUSTOMER] a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use Background IP solely as embedded in the Deliverables.

**Note:** Separate Deliverables from Background IP cleanly. Do not let Vendor claim ownership of your data or data-derived outputs.

---

## Governing Law & Venue — Preferred
**Placeholders:** [JURISDICTION], [VENUE].

> This Agreement is governed by the laws of [JURISDICTION], without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in [VENUE] for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**Note:** Prefer your home jurisdiction and courts over mandatory arbitration in an inconvenient forum. Watch for fee-shifting riders.
