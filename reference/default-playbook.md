# Default Negotiation Playbook (Template)

> This is a **generic starter playbook** for demonstration. Replace every position with your organization's actual, counsel-approved standards before relying on it. The `playbook-check` skill uses this file only when no organization-specific playbook is supplied.

Each topic defines three tiers:
- **Standard** — our opening/preferred position.
- **Fallback** — the worst we will accept without escalation.
- **Red line** — must escalate to a human attorney; do not accept.

| Topic | Standard | Fallback | Red line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Limitation of liability** | Mutual cap at 12 months' fees; carve-outs for confidentiality, IP infringement, and indemnity are mutual | Cap at 24 months' fees; limited one-sided carve-outs | Uncapped liability for us, or no cap at all |
| **Consequential damages** | Mutual waiver of consequential/indirect damages | Mutual waiver with narrow exceptions | One-sided waiver against us only |
| **Indemnification** | Mutual, capped, tied to the liability cap where possible | IP and third-party-claim indemnity from vendor to us | Broad, uncapped indemnity flowing from us |
| **Termination for convenience** | Either party, 30–60 days' notice | We may terminate for convenience with notice | No exit for us except for cause |
| **Termination for cause** | Mutual, with 30-day cure period | 15-day cure | No cure period; immediate termination against us |
| **Payment terms** | Net 45; no auto price increases without consent | Net 30; capped annual escalator (≤5%) | Unilateral price increases; net <15 |
| **Auto-renewal** | Manual renewal, or auto-renew with ≥60 days' notice window | Auto-renew with 30 days' notice | Evergreen with no practical exit |
| **IP ownership** | We own deliverables/work product; vendor retains pre-existing IP under license | Joint ownership of deliverables | Vendor owns our work product or our data-derived IP |
| **Confidentiality** | Mutual, 3–5 year term, standard exclusions | Mutual, longer term | One-way against us; residuals clause favoring vendor |
| **Data protection** | DPA required; breach notice ≤72h; encryption at rest and in transit | Breach notice ≤7 days | No security obligations or breach notice |
| **Warranties** | Vendor warrants performance to spec and non-infringement | Limited performance warranty | Full "AS IS" disclaimer against us |
| **Insurance** | Commercially reasonable coverage (GL, cyber, E&O) matched to risk | Reduced limits | No insurance requirement for high-risk vendor |
| **Assignment** | No assignment without consent; carve-out for internal reorganizations | Consent not unreasonably withheld | Free assignment to competitors |
| **Governing law / venue** | Our home jurisdiction; courts (not mandatory arbitration) | Neutral jurisdiction | Inconvenient foreign venue; loser-pays fee-shifting |
| **Publicity** | No use of our name/logo without written consent | Logo use with prior approval per instance | Blanket publicity rights |
