# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (Sample v1)

*This is a fictional sample contract for demonstrating the plugin. It is not legal advice and not a usable template.*

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between **Acme Corp.** ("Customer") and **Nimbus Solutions, Inc.** ("Vendor").

## 1. Services
Vendor will provide the services described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW").

## 2. Term
The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter, the Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment
Customer will pay the fees stated in each SOW. Invoices are due net forty-five (45) days. Fees will not increase during the initial term.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care and use it only to perform under this Agreement. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination. Standard exclusions (public information, independently developed, rightfully received) apply.

## 5. Intellectual Property
Deliverables created specifically for Customer are assigned to Customer. Vendor retains its pre-existing materials and grants Customer a license to use them as embedded in the Deliverables.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. EXCLUDED CLAIMS MEANS BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY AND INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS.

## 7. Indemnification
Vendor will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Deliverables infringe intellectual property rights.

## 8. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice. Customer may terminate any SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Delaware.
